## G2B-1: Primary Analysis

The treatment was associated with a reduction in mortality (OR = 2.45, 95% CI: 1.23-4.87).
Under the assumption of no unmeasured confounders, the causal effect estimate suggests a benefit.

### Limitations
- This is an observational study; causal inference requires the assumption of exchangeability.
- Unmeasured confounding cannot be ruled out.
- Results should be interpreted with caution given the study design limitations.
